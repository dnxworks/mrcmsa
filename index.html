Booking rules

  1. 3 days per week. Each person, club or organization can book the court for a maximum of 3 days in a week. After that, the next days go to other users.
  2. No-show, late or last-minute cancel. If you do not use your booking, cancel at the last minute (less than 3 hours before), or arrive more than 15 minutes late without a good reason, you cannot book for 3 days.
  3. Weekend evenings. Saturday and Sunday evening sessions are only for events and programs of University Bodies (UB), with MRC approval.
  4. Weekday evenings. Monday to Friday evening sessions rotate between Volleyball, Netball and Futsal (see the table below).
  5. WhatsApp Community. Volleyball, Netball and Futsal groups are joined in one WhatsApp Community. Booking news, schedules and announcements are sent there.
  6. Rotation is fair. If you already used the court more than 3 days in a week, give other users priority on the next day.
